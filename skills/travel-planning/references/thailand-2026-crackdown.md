# Thailand 2026 Crackdown — Research Summary

**Date researched:** June 15, 2026
**Sources:** Tilleke & Gibbins, Reuters, ASEAN Now, Straits Times, Thai Embassy

## Key Changes (May 19, 2026 — Cabinet Approved)

| Rule | Before | Now |
|------|--------|-----|
| Visa-exempt stay | 60 days | **30 days** |
| Eligible countries | 93 | **54** |
| Visa on Arrival | 31 countries | **4 only** (Azerbaijan, Belarus, Serbia, India) |
| Multiple exemptions | Overlapping OK | **Single privilege per country** |

**Status:** Approved in principle, NOT yet in effect. Takes effect 15 days after Government Gazette publication.

## Proof of Funds

- **Amount:** 20,000 Baht per person (~$550 USD) or 40,000 Baht per family
- **Enforcement:** Random checks at immigration — pull people out of line
- **Format:** Cash preferred. Bank statements NOT accepted.
- **Source:** Thai Embassy official requirement + Tim Travel Taste video (Jun 2026)

## Overstay Penalties

| Penalty | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Fine | 500 Baht/day, capped at 20,000 Baht (~$550) |
| Jail | Up to 2 years (statutory) |
| Re-entry ban | 1-10 years depending on overstay length |
| Arrested while overstaying | Mandatory deportation + 5-10 year ban |

## Unruly Tourist Crackdown (May 2026)

PM Anutin Charnvirakul ordered nationwide enforcement:
- **Zero tolerance** in Phuket — maximum penalties, immediate visa revocation
- **No driving without valid license** — court appearance without exception
- **Cultural respect enforced** — no bullying locals, no disrespectful behavior
- **Nominee businesses investigated** — foreigners using tourist visas to work

## Mandatory Health Insurance (Proposed)

Thailand considering mandatory health insurance for all foreign visitors. Not yet implemented but being reviewed. Get travel insurance regardless.
